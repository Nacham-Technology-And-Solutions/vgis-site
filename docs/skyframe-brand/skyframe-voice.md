# Voice & messaging

## How Skyframe sounds
**Show, don't promise.** Our whole product exists because buyers are tired of "ideas" and "promises". Our words follow the same rule: concrete, visual, specific.

| Principle | Do | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Concrete** | "Walk from plot 10A to the gate." | "Experience next-generation immersion." |
| **Confident, calm** | "See it before it's built." | "The most revolutionary VR ever!!!" |
| **Buyer-first** | "Know the size of your kitchen before you pay." | "Our proprietary pipeline leverages…" (save tech for the tech section) |
| **Simple** | "No PC. No cables. Just a headset and a code." | Acronym soup: ASTC, LOD, MDM in sales copy. |
| **Honest** | "Finishes shown are illustrative." | Implying renders are final specification. |

### Tone by audience
- **Developers & investors:** businesslike, numbers-led, short sentences. Lead with outcomes: sell faster, cut rework, reach diaspora capital.
- **Buyers:** warm, reassuring, second person ("your home", "your plot").
- **In the app:** instructional and short. Verbs first: "Enter your Property Code", "Choose a finish", "Walk to the kitchen".

### House style
- British/Nigerian English spelling: *colour, customise, neighbourhood, programme*.
- Numbers: ₦350,000; 1:1 scale; Meta Quest 3; 10 developers.
- "Property Code" is a product term: always capitalised.
- "Off-plan" and "bare plot", hyphenated as shown.
- Credit line, exactly: **A partnership between Arkfield Realty & VGIS.** Both partners carry equal weight; never list one alone.

## Boilerplate

**One line**
Skyframe XR lets buyers walk through off-plan homes at real size, on a Meta Quest 3, before a single brick is laid.

**Short (≈50 words)**
Skyframe XR is a real estate technology platform that turns architectural plans into immersive, walkable virtual spaces. Developers give buyers a unique Property Code; buyers open it in the Skyframe XR app on Meta Quest 3, anywhere in the world. A partnership between Arkfield Realty & VGIS.

**Long (≈100 words)**
Skyframe XR helps real estate developers sell off-plan property and bare plots faster. Developers send their architectural drawings; the Skyframe team turns them into photorealistic, high-performance virtual models. Buyers enter a unique Property Code in the Skyframe XR app on Meta Quest 3 and walk the estate block by block, step inside their unit at 1:1 scale and try wall colours, tiles and kitchen styles instantly. No PC, no cables, no complicated setup, and it works on everyday internet connections. Skyframe XR is a partnership between Arkfield Realty & VGIS. Walk Through Tomorrow, Today.

## Words we use / avoid
| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| walk through, step inside, see, stand in | metaverse, digital twin (in buyer copy), cutting-edge |
| real size, 1:1 scale | life-like (vague) |
| Property Code | access key, PIN, password |
| headset | goggles, glasses (v1 term; retired) |
| buyers, developers, agents | users (in marketing copy) |
| off-plan, bare plot | unbuilt, empty land |
