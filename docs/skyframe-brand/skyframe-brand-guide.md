# Skyframe XR — Brand Guide

**Walk Through Tomorrow, Today.**

Skyframe XR is real estate technology that lets buyers walk through off-plan homes and bare plots, at real size, before a single brick is laid. Version 2.0 runs standalone on Meta Quest 3 and opens any developer's project from the cloud with a unique Property Code.

Skyframe XR is **a partnership between Arkfield Realty & VGIS**, credited equally.

This guide is the single reference for how Skyframe XR looks, sounds and is applied: in the pitch deck, on the website, in the Quest app, at the sales gallery, on social and in print. It is built from the brand's own material: the Aug 2025 pitch deck, the *SKYFRAME BY ARKFIELD* brochure, the three logo files, the cover page and the Product Bible v2.0. Where those sources were silent or inconsistent, this guide makes a decision and says so.

---

## 1. Brand foundation

### What we are
A B2B platform that turns architectural plans into immersive, walkable virtual spaces, so real estate developers sell faster, avoid costly rework and reach buyers anywhere, including the diaspora.

### The promise
Buyers stop imagining. They **see what they're buying, right where it will be**, and decide with confidence instead of guesswork.

### Brand idea: *proof before the brick*
Every Skyframe touchpoint replaces a promise with an experience. Our visual language comes from the moment a 2D blueprint becomes a space you can stand in: precise line, warm daylight, one bold orange signal.

### Personality
| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| **Precise.** Real scale, real layouts, real numbers. | Hype. No "revolutionary metaverse" talk. |
| **Confident.** We show rather than explain. | Arrogant or salesy. |
| **Grounded.** Built for Nigerian developers, buyers and connectivity. | Generic Silicon Valley tech. |
| **Welcoming.** First-time VR users feel at ease within a minute. | Gadget-obsessed or jargon-heavy. |

### Audiences
- **Developers** (Enterprise) and **architectural firms** (Pro): the buyers of the platform. Speak to speed of sale, fewer changes, global reach.
- **Off-plan, bare-plot and diaspora buyers**: the people in the headset. Speak to certainty, scale, choice of finishes.
- **Sales agents and realtors**: the presenters. Speak to an easy, confident demo.
- **Investors and partners** (REDAN, NIA): speak to platform scale and proof.

## 2. Name

- Write it **Skyframe XR**: capital S, lower-case "kyframe", a space, capital XR. For this version: **Skyframe XR 2.0**.
- Short form in running text after first mention: **Skyframe**.
- In the logo the name is set in caps (SKYFRAME). That is a logotype, not the spelling: never type "SKYFRAME XR" or "SkyFrame" in sentences.
- Retired variants: SkyFrame, SkyFrame XR, Sky-Frame, Skyframe-XR, SkyframeXR (except in handles and URLs, e.g. @skyframexr).
- Never "Skyframe VR" or "Skyframe AR": XR is the umbrella.

## 3. Logo

Skyframe has one mark: the **headset-frame**. The M of SKYFRAME is replaced by a VR headset drawn in a single rounded stroke, with **XR** in Frame Orange inside the visor. The headset is a window: it frames the future.

### Lockups
| Lockup | File | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Horizontal, black** (primary) | `assets/Logos/skyframe-xr-horizontal-black.png` | Default on Paper, White and light photography. Website header, deck, documents, signage. |
| **Horizontal, white** | `assets/Logos/skyframe-xr-horizontal-white.png` | On Ink, Charcoal and dark photography. The headset is open (ground shows through), XR stays orange. |
| **Badge** | `assets/Logos/skyframe-xr-badge.png` | App icon, social avatar, favicon, stickers, headset strap, watermark. Works on any ground. |
| **Stacked** | `assets/Logos/skyframe-xr-stacked.png` / `-white.png` | Square formats where the horizontal is too wide: merch, event backdrops, splash screens. |

### Clear space
Keep a clear zone around the logo equal to the **height of the letter E** on all sides. Around the badge, keep one quarter of its diameter clear. Nothing (text, edges, other logos) enters that zone.

### Minimum size
| Lockup | Digital | Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 72 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Badge | 32 px (favicon: 16 px allowed) | 8 mm |

Below 120 px, use the badge instead of the horizontal logo.

### Backgrounds
- **Paper / White / Concrete / Orange-tint** → black horizontal.
- **Ink / Charcoal / dark photos** → white horizontal.
- **Frame Orange** → black horizontal only; the orange XR will blend in, so on solid orange prefer the **badge** or move the logo onto a Paper panel.
- **Busy photography** → place the logo on a Paper or Ink panel, or use the badge.

### Don't
- Don't re-type SKYFRAME in another font or redraw the headset.
- Don't recolour: the letters are black or white only; XR is always Frame Orange; the badge ring is always orange.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or glows.
- Don't use the black logo on dark grounds (it disappears; a known issue in the source files).
- Don't separate the headset from the word, except as the **badge**.
- Don't put the logo inside a sentence or lock other names to it. Partner credit sits below or beside, never inside the clear space.

> **To do:** the source logos are 1000 px PNGs. Commission vector masters (SVG, PDF, AI) before large-format print, and confirm the wordmark font (it matches **Montserrat ExtraBold**).

## 4. Colour

Three brand colours carry Skyframe: **Ink**, **Paper** and **Frame Orange**. Charcoal and the neutrals support them.

| Colour | Hex | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Frame Orange** | `#FEA000` | 254 160 0 | 0 40 100 0 | Signature. Highlights, pills, primary buttons, the XR. |
| **Ink** | `#111111` | 17 17 17 | 0 0 0 95 | Text, logo, outlines. |
| **Paper** | `#FFFBF7` | 255 251 247 | 0 1 2 0 | Default page ground (warm off-white from the deck). |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 | Cards on Paper; print stock. |
| **Charcoal** | `#292929` | 41 41 41 | 0 0 0 90 | Dark bands, feature bars, footers. |
| **Concrete** | `#EDEBE8` | 237 235 232 | 0 1 2 7 | Architectural backgrounds (brochure). |
| **Orange Deep** | `#9A5A00` | 154 90 0 | 0 42 100 40 | Orange *text* on light grounds. |
| **Orange Tint** | `#FFF1D6` | 255 241 214 | 0 5 16 0 | Soft callout panels. |
| **Ink Muted** | `#6B645C` | 107 100 92 | 0 7 14 58 | Secondary text. |

Pantone: closest match for Frame Orange is **Pantone 137 C**. Verify against a printed swatch before any print run; the source files gave no official codes.

### Proportion
Roughly **60 % Paper/White, 30 % Ink/Charcoal, 10 % Frame Orange**. Orange is the signal, so it works best scarce: one orange element per view should draw the eye first. Full-orange slides or panels are allowed as section openers (as in *Market Opportunity* and *Business Model*), never two in a row.

### Accessibility rules
- **Ink on Orange** is the only text pairing on orange (9.2 : 1). Never white on orange.
- **Never orange text on Paper or White** (2 : 1). Use `orange-deep` (#9A5A00) for orange words and links on light grounds.
- On Ink and Charcoal, Frame Orange text is fine (9.2 : 1 and 7.1 : 1).
- Status colours (`success`, `error`) are for states only, never decoration.

### Dark theme
The Quest app and the sales-gallery TV use the **Ink** theme: Ink ground, Paper text, Frame Orange accents. Dark is the default inside the headset (less glare, better contrast against renders).

## 5. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weights | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Display** | **Montserrat** | ExtraBold 800, Bold 700 | It is the face of the wordmark and the brochure headings, so headlines and logo speak with one voice. |
| **Body & UI** | **Inter** | Regular 400, SemiBold 600 | Highly legible on screens and in the headset at small sizes. |
| **Property Codes** | **JetBrains Mono** | SemiBold 600 | Unambiguous characters for codes buyers type. |

All three are free on Google Fonts and licensed for web, print and apps.

**Note on the pitch deck:** the Aug 2025 deck was set in *Telegraf* and *Neue Montreal* (Pangram Pangram). They look close to this pairing but need a paid commercial licence. Keep using them only if you buy the licence; otherwise Montserrat + Inter replace them everywhere.

### Type scale
| Style | Size / line | Weight | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| display-xl | 72 / 72 | 800, −2 % tracking | Covers, title slides |
| display-lg | 56 / 60 | 800 | Slide titles, hero |
| heading-1 | 40 / 44 | 800 | Page titles |
| heading-2 | 28 / 34 | 700 | Sub-sections |
| heading-3 | 20 / 26 | 700 | Card titles |
| eyebrow | 12 / 16 | 700, +18 % tracking, UPPERCASE | Kickers |
| body-lg | 18 / 28 | 400 | Lead text |
| body | 16 / 24 | 400 | Running text |
| body-sm | 14 / 20 | 400 | Card text, disclaimers |
| label | 14 / 20 | 600 | Buttons, pills |
| caption | 12 / 16 | 500 | Sources, footnotes |
| property-code | 28 / 36 | 600 mono, +20 % tracking | Property Codes |

### Rules
- Headlines in **Title Case or sentence case**, tight and bold. Use the **orange highlight bar** (a Frame Orange block behind one word or line, Ink text on it) for one emphasis per page, as on the deck cover.
- Eyebrows are the only all-caps text besides the logo.
- Left-align text. Centre only on covers and single-statement slides.
- Use ₦ for Naira and write amounts in full in investor material (₦350,000/month).
- In-headset UI: never below 20 px equivalent at 1 m; prefer body-lg and heading-3.
