# Graphic language & applications

## Signature elements
Five elements make a layout read as Skyframe, even with the logo covered.

1. **The orange highlight bar.** A solid Frame Orange block behind one word or line of a headline, Ink text on top (the deck's "Pitch Deck **Presentation**"). One per page.
2. **Orange pill labels.** Rounded `radius-pill` tags in Frame Orange with Ink `label` text, sitting on top of outlined cards or above headings. A Charcoal pill is the alternate.
3. **The outlined card.** White card, 1.5 px Ink outline (`border-outline`, `line-strong`), `radius-lg` corners, with a pill label overlapping its top edge. Flat: no shadow.
4. **The halftone fade.** A grid of Ink dots that shrink from one edge to nothing (the cover page). It stands for a plan dissolving into reality. Use as a background on covers, section openers and social posts; keep it behind text-free areas.
5. **The headset frame.** The rounded visor shape from the logo, used as a crop or window for photography and renders (one per layout).

Supporting: numbered step markers (Ink or Orange circles with ONE / TWO / THREE or 1, 2, 3) and the dark Charcoal feature bar.

## Layout
- 12-column grid; margins `space-12` (mobile) / `space-16` (desktop); slide margins 48 px at 1920 × 1080.
- Generous white space on Paper. One idea per slide or section.
- Logo top-left on slides and pages; credit line and URL in the footer.

## Imagery
**Photography**
- Real people, predominantly Nigerian and diaspora buyers, in a headset, reacting with genuine curiosity. Natural light, warm tones.
- The sales-gallery moment: a guide, a wearer and a shared TV screen.
- Avoid: generic stock "VR gamer" shots, neon cyberpunk lighting, blue-purple gradients.

**Renders**
- Photoreal Skyframe renders in daylight. Always label as illustrative where finishes or furniture are not final.
- The brochure's **line-art-to-render** treatment (an architectural wireframe resolving into a finished building) is a hero technique: use it for "blueprint to VR" stories.

**Backgrounds**
- Paper, Concrete, or a faint architectural grid. Ink for app and gallery screens.

## Iconography
- Line icons, **1.5–2 px stroke, rounded caps and joins**, matching the headset stroke in the logo. Recommended set: Lucide (open source).
- Icons in Ink or Paper; Frame Orange only inside a filled circle (orange circle, Ink icon), as in the deck's feature bullets.
- No emoji, no 3D clip-art icons.

## Data & charts
- Bars and the main series in Frame Orange; comparison series in Ink; gridlines in `line`.
- Always cite the source in `caption` style under the chart.

## Applications

### Pitch deck (16:9)
Paper ground, logo top-left, Montserrat ExtraBold titles, one orange highlight bar or one full-orange opener per section. End slide: master tagline, contact row on Charcoal.

### Website
Paper ground, black horizontal logo in the header, primary CTA as an orange pill ("Book a demo"). Hero: master tagline + headset photography. Footer on Charcoal with the white logo and the credit line.

### Quest app
Ink theme. Badge on the splash screen, then the horizontal white logo in the app shell. Property Code entry on Charcoal in JetBrains Mono, grouped in fours (e.g. K7QM-4TZP). Comfort and disclaimer screens use body-lg on Ink. Developer branding (logo, colours, welcome screen) sits inside the Skyframe shell; the Skyframe logo stays visible.

### Sales gallery & events
Pull-up banner: halftone fade top, horizontal black logo, "Walk Through Tomorrow, Today.", credit line at the base. TV mirror screen: thin Ink frame with the badge bottom-right. Headset straps or stickers: the badge.

### Social
Avatar: the badge. Posts: Paper or Ink ground, one headline, one image, the badge in a corner. Handle: @skyframexr.

### Stationery
Business card 85 × 55 mm: front Ink with the white horizontal logo; back Paper with name, role, phone, email and the credit line. Email signature: name, role, "Skyframe XR · Walk Through Tomorrow, Today.", website.

## Co-branding
- **With Arkfield Realty & VGIS:** credit line in text, or their logos at equal size to each other, below or to the right of the Skyframe logo, separated by a hairline, at no more than 70 % of the Skyframe logo's height.
- **With client developers:** "Powered by Skyframe XR" + badge on the developer's materials; inside the app, the developer's logo appears on the welcome screen while the Skyframe shell stays.
- Don't show partner or client logos (REDAN, NIA, Besitz, LOI signatories) until the relationship is confirmed in writing.
